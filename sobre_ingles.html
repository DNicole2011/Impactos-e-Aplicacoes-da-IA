Artificial Intelligence is the field of computer science focused on studying and developing systems. It creates machines and software capable of mimicking human behavior in decision-making and tasks, ranging from simple to complex.

The architecture behind data

No magic: AI is pure mathematics and statistics. It analyzes billions of data points using algorithms to identify patterns and calculate the optimal answer in milliseconds.

How it is present in everyday life

Have you ever thought about just typing your favorite colors and having AI build the perfect outfits for you to buy? A US brand revenue reached 295 billion dollars with just this feature on their website! AI is already in our daily lives making everything simpler.

How Artificial Intelligence models work can be summarized in four steps:

1

Web Scraping

Companies collect data from public websites, books, magazines, forums, and articles that will serve as the basis for training the models.

2

Learning

AI constantly analyzes and learns from the provided data, identifying various patterns within these large volumes of data. This process is continuous, meaning the AI ​​is constantly learning and developing.

3

Deep Learning

AI utilizes neural networks to emulate the human brain and process complex data, providing solutions to presented problems.

4

Final Result

At this point, AI has learned to mimic human behavior, identify patterns, and can provide fast and concise answers to various problems and questions.

What boosted AI?

Logic and Reasoning: A great example of logic is binary code. George Boole created a logical system where anything could be reduced to True (1) or False (0). In 1937, this was applied to electrical circuits: On (1) or Off (0). Later, building on this knowledge, binary became the "language" that computers understand.

Data Volume: The advancement of global digitalization generates a massive amount of data daily. This volume has become the essential fuel for training complex machine learning models, a process driven by hardware evolution, which allowed everything to be processed even faster.
