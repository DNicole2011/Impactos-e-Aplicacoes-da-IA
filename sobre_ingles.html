Artificial Intelligence is the field of computer science focused on the study and development of systems. It creates machines and programs capable of reproducing human behavior in decisions and tasks, from simple to complex ones.
Generative AI

The central focus of research and media in 2024. It creates original content in an automated way, generating texts, images, videos, and various media.

What can it do

Simulates complex human tasks and decisions. Processes language, vision, data, and recommendations, driving autonomous cars and operating in networks and search engines.

Daily Life

Present in daily routine since the 1950s, acting in voice, searches, networks, and autonomous transport, provoking fundamental debates on social ethics.

Learning

Uses machine learning with large databases and applies algorithms to extract deep knowledge, converting machines into fully autonomous agents.

How Artificial Intelligence models work can be summarized in four steps:

1

Web Scraping

Companies collect data from public websites, books, magazines, forums, and articles that will serve as the basis for training the models.

2

Learning

AI analyzes and learns from the provided data, identifying various patterns within these large volumes of information.

3

Deep Learning

AI utilizes neural networks to emulate the human brain and process complex data, providing solutions to presented problems.

4

Final Result

At this point, AI has learned to mimic human behavior, identify patterns, and can provide fast and concise answers to various problems and questions.

What boosted AI

GPUs

Initially, their functions were focused on graphics; they split complex problems into multiple tasks and work on them simultaneously, instead of one by one like CPUs. With the need for deep learning in AI, they were used precisely because of this processing capability.

TPUs

Their goal when developed was to accelerate machine learning workloads. They operate on tensors, excelling at processing large data volumes and efficiently executing complex neural networks, enabling fast training and inference times.

Information Age

The "fuel" of AI. We have a massive volume of information (texts, images, videos, etc.). This is the amount of data AI needs to handle when you ask something; hence, it is highly necessary for it to develop continuously to handle all of this and provide a concrete and correct response.

What to expect from the future of AI

Integration into daily life

In the future, it is expected that this technology can be implemented into daily routines, making it present in everyday life, studying, working, and creating.

AI Agents +

More personalization in each agent, integrated into every system and situation. They will be able to understand routine, patterns, and much more.

AI & Robotics

Humanoid robots. In the future, gradually making it possible to combine Artificial Intelligence with physical hardware, assisting in practical and even complex tasks.
